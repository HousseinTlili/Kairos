**Status (2026-10-29):** **built** on `m14/island` (stacked after `m13/rich-answers`), at the
product owner's request: *"a small version of Elissa"* in Kairos, chosen as **an island pill**.

**Since 2026-10-31 (D-034):** the pill is a full-width top bar that hides behind maximized windows
and comes back when the mouse touches the top of the screen. What follows describes the pill as
first built.

## 1. Goal and scope

Kairos is always there, but today it only shows itself when called (the quick box, Ctrl+Alt+K)
or in its main window. The island makes it **present**: a small pill at the top of the screen,
like Elissa's, that shows at a glance what Kairos is doing and opens it in one click. When the
Master says *"Kairos"*, the Master's own standing Kairos slides in from the side of the screen
(the avatar wish from 2026-10-26).

- **The pill** — always on top, top-centre of the screen, about 300 × 40 px: the seal (its four
  states: idle, listening, working, speaking), one line of status, and a microphone.
  - Status in words: *"At your service"*, *"Listening…"*, the request being worked on
    (*"Opening Notepad…"*), *"Kairos asks your leave"* (gold, when an approval waits), *"Done —
    Notepad is open"* for a few seconds after an answer.
  - **Click** → the quick box opens just under the pill (the M8 quick box, unchanged).
    **Click while an approval waits** → the main window opens on that conversation's card.
    **Microphone** → push-to-talk, as Ctrl+Alt+J. **Right-click** → Open Kairos, Hide the island
    for an hour, Settings.
- **The wake panel** — on the wake word, a panel slides in from the right edge: the standing
  Kairos (the Master's video, looping), what he heard (the transcript), and the answer's first
  lines while it is spoken. It slides away 4 s after the answer ends, with its × or Esc, or 9 s
  after a wake that heard nothing. (Not on "a click outside": Windows doesn't tell an overlay
  reliably, Elissa's lesson.) No wake word, no panel: it never appears by itself.
- **Out of the way** — the pill hides while a full-screen app (a game, a film, a presentation)
  is in front, and while Kairos captures the screen (as the quick box does, M8 §4). It never takes
  the keyboard focus from the app the Master is using.

Out of scope (Elissa's, not Kairos's): system widgets in the pill, wallpaper, clipboard history,
a launcher, moving or tiling windows. The pill is not draggable in this milestone (top-centre or
top-right, chosen in Settings).

## 2. What exists and what Elissa taught (read 2026-10-29)

- **Kairos already has** the quick box window (`quick.rs`, `QuickApp.tsx`: one question, the
  latest answer, Esc to hide), the presence states (`shell/presence.ts`, the seal), the wake-word
  event (`wake` with `{spoken}`, M9 Step 9), run events, approvals, and the charter's tokens and
  fonts. The island adds a window and reuses these; the quick box and the main window don't change.
- **The Master's art:** `Videos/` (not committed) holds three Google Flow clips of the standing
  Kairos — `1.mp4` and `2.mp4` (720 × 1280, 15 s) and `Butler_standing_on_dark_floor_1080p_1.mp4`
  (1080 × 1920, 8 s): H.264, 24 fps, portrait, the butler on a dark floor. No alpha channel, so
  the panel is a dark card around the video, not a cut-out over the desktop; the dark floor sits
  well on the charter's charcoal.
- **Elissa's lessons** (`Elissa/docs/PROGRESS.md`, `src-tauri/src/core/window.rs`):
  - a transparent window larger than its pill must be made click-through by hand: Windows can only
    make a whole window click-through, so Elissa polls the cursor every 16 ms. **Kairos avoids it:
    the pill window is the pill's own size**, and the panel is a solid card;
  - an animation running at the display's refresh rate in a transparent, always-on-top window cost
    **about a third of a CPU core** (Elissa's idle mascot at 165 Hz). **Kairos's pill is still
    unless its state changes**, its pulses are CSS on a few elements, and the video plays only
    while the panel is on screen;
  - a hotkey-opened overlay often doesn't get focus from Windows, so "click outside" is detected
    from the mouse, not from a blur event;
  - DPI and a second monitor need testing at 100, 125, 150 and 200 %.

## 3. Decisions

- **D-029 — The Kairos island: a pill window the size of the pill, and a wake panel with the
  Master's standing Kairos — Proposed.**
  - Two small windows created by Kairos: `island` (transparent, no decorations, always on top, not
    in the taskbar, never focused on show) and `presence-panel` (the wake panel, same flags).
  - The pill only shows state and opens things; every action still goes through Kairos's existing
    paths (the quick box, the main window, push-to-talk), so the rules and approvals are unchanged.
  - Full-screen detection uses Windows's own `SHQueryUserNotificationState` (busy, full-screen
    Direct3D, presentation mode): one more feature, `Win32_UI_Shell`, on the `windows-sys` crate
    Kairos already uses. No new crate.
  - The videos are the Master's own art: re-encoded once, with the `ffmpeg` Hermes already
    bundles, to 540 × 960 H.264 without sound (about 1–2 MB each; WebView2 plays H.264), and kept
    in the app's assets. The originals stay in `Videos/`.
- **No new dependency.**

## 4. Steps

### Step 1 — Spec and choices
This file; D-029 proposed; the product owner's answers to §6.
**Check:** the product owner reads it.

### Step 2 — The pill window
Rust: create `island` at start (if enabled), sized to the pill, top-centre (or top-right) of the
monitor under the cursor when first shown, re-placed when monitors or scaling change; not
focusable on show; hidden in full-screen apps (checked every 2 s, and on foreground change) and
during screen capture; tray item "Show the island".
**Check:** Rust tests for placement (scaling, negative monitor origins, two monitors) and the
full-screen states; **(on the PC)** 100/150 % scaling and a second screen; a game or a full-screen
video hides it.

### Step 3 — What the pill shows and does
The seal, the status line (from run events, voice phase, approvals), the mic; click → quick box
under the pill; approval waiting → main window on that card; right-click menu. Settings ▸ Kairos ▸
Island: show the island, where, show the wake panel.
**Check:** TS tests for the status line ("an approval outranks a running task", "the done line
fades after 4 s"); screenshots dark and light; CPU at idle with the pill on screen under 1 % of a
core (Task Manager / `measure` script, as Elissa's).

### Step 4 — The wake panel
On `wake`: slide in from the right (CSS transform, 220 ms), loop the idle clip, show the transcript
and the answer's first lines; the clip for "speaking" while the answer is read (§6); slide out 4 s
after the answer ends, on Esc, or on a click outside; the video is paused and unloaded when hidden.
**Check:** TS tests for when the panel opens and closes; a screenshot; CPU while it plays and
after it hides; **(on the PC)** say "Kairos, what time is it?".

### Step 5 — Acceptance (product owner, on the PC)
`docs/milestones/M14-acceptance.md`: a day with the island on — glanceable, never in the way, the
panel on the wake word, nothing slower.

## 5. Definition of done

Steps 2–4 built with their checks shown; CPU at idle under 1 % of a core; D-029 accepted; Step 5
run by the product owner.

## 6. Choices for the product owner

1. **Where the pill sits:** top-centre (Elissa's place, so the two can't share it if both run) or
   top-right.
2. **Which clip when:** the panel can loop one clip for everything, or use one for listening and
   another while Kairos speaks. The three clips look alike at a glance (the same standing pose);
   the product owner knows which moves how.
3. **On at start:** whether the island shows from the moment Kairos starts (Elissa's choice) or
   only after it is switched on in Settings.

# M16 — Themes and patterns

Implementation spec. Read `CLAUDE.md` first.

**Status (2026-10-29):** spec only, on `m16/themes` (stacked after `m15/island-panel`), at the
product owner's request: *"multiple themes with different colors and patterns; I'll later send
images for the patterns"*. Choices made the same day: **Carthage, Midnight and Ember** besides
**Charter**, **each in dark and light**. D-031 is Proposed. The pattern images are to come.

## 1. Goal and scope

Settings ▸ Kairos ▸ **Appearance**: pick a theme, a light or dark mode, and a pattern, and the whole
of Kairos changes at once: the main window, the quick box, the island and its panel, the wake
panel, the title bar, tables and diagrams.

| Theme | Dark | Light | Character |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Charter** (today's) | charcoal, gold, crimson seal | vellum, charcoal sidebar | the illuminated charter |
| **Carthage** | near-black aubergine, Tyrian purple, gold | parchment, Tyrian purple accents | matches Elissa and the Master's Carthaginian projects |
| **Midnight** | deep navy, silver, ice-blue | pale slate, navy accents | calm, cool |
| **Ember** | black, crimson red, ember orange | warm ash, crimson accents | strong, warm |

- **Mode:** follow Windows (today's behaviour), always dark, always light.
- **Pattern:** each theme has its own default; the Master can choose another or none. Charter's
  diaper stays; the **Master's pattern images** are added as they arrive (§5).
- The fonts (EB Garamond, UnifrakturCook, Segoe UI) and the seal's shape stay; the seal and the
  gold follow the theme's accent.

Out of scope: a theme editor (picking each colour), per-window themes, importing themes.

## 2. How Kairos's colours work today (read 2026-10-29)

Every colour in `global.css` derives from nine base tokens on `:root` (`--charcoal`, `--hearth`,
`--ember`, `--iron`, `--gold`, `--gules`, `--vert`, `--vellum`, `--ash`) through names such as
`--bg`, `--surface`, `--fg`, `--gilt` (352 uses of `var(--…)`); the light version redefines them
under `@media (prefers-color-scheme: light)`. The diaper pattern is `--pattern` (an SVG data URI) on
`.main`. The title bar is coloured by `titlebar.rs` (DWM caption, text and border colours). Mermaid
diagrams read the tokens when drawn (M13). So a theme is mostly **a new set of the nine**.

## 3. Decisions

- **D-031 — Themes are sets of the nine base tokens, chosen on `<html>`; patterns are tiled images
  — Proposed.**
  - `html[data-theme="carthage"][data-mode="dark"]` (and light) redefine the nine tokens and
    `--pattern`; nothing else in the stylesheet changes. "Follow Windows" keeps the media query.
  - The choice is a Kairos setting; every window applies it at start and on a `theme-changed`
    event, and Rust recolours the title bar.
  - Patterns: the Master's images (PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG) bundled in `app/src/assets/patterns/`,
    tiled at low opacity behind the main view, as the diaper is now; each theme picks a default.
  - **Readability is tested, not eyeballed:** a test computes the contrast of text on background for
    every theme and mode and fails under WCAG AA (4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text and
    borders that matter).
- **No new dependency.**

## 5. What the pattern images should be

So they tile well and stay quiet behind text:
- **Seamless tiles** (the right edge continues into the left, the bottom into the top), square,
  about 200–600 px; or SVG.
- **Low contrast on their own**: Kairos fades them to a few percent of opacity, so line drawings or
  simple shapes work best; a photograph won't.
- One colour or transparent background is ideal (Kairos tints them to the theme); if not, say
  which theme each is meant for.
